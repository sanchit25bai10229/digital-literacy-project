The Student's Social Media Checklist

The Do’s

Do Curate Your "Digital Footprint": Regularly Google yourself to see what information is public. Use platforms like LinkedIn to highlight your academic projects, such as coding scripts or technical models, to build a professional brand.

Do Use Strong Privacy Settings: Check your security permissions on every platform. Ensure that personal photos and life updates are restricted to "Friends Only" rather than being accessible to the general public or recruiters.

Do Think Before You Post: Pause and consider how a post might be interpreted by a future employer or an admissions officer. If a post could be seen as controversial or offensive, it’s better left in the drafts.

Do Network Meaningfully: Use social media to follow industry leaders, join professional groups, and engage with content related to your field of study. This can lead to internship opportunities and career insights.

Do Fact-Check Before Sharing: In an era of rapid misinformation, verify the source of an article or "news" post before sharing it. Being a responsible digital citizen involves promoting accuracy.

The Don’ts

Don't Post Academic Materials: Avoid sharing photos of exams, specific assignment prompts, or copyrighted lecture slides. This can inadvertently lead to charges of academic dishonesty or intellectual property theft.

Don't Badmouth Institutions or People: Avoid venting about professors, classmates, or previous employers. Publicly criticizing your university or workplace can make you appear difficult to work with.

Don't Post While Emotional: Avoid "venting" or posting during moments of high stress or anger. What feels like a relief in the moment can become a permanent mark on your reputation once you've cooled down.

Don't Overshare Your Location: Avoid posting "live" updates of your exact location for safety reasons. It is much safer to post photos of an event or a trip after you have already left the venue.

Don't Ignore the "Tagging" Settings: Don't let others define your digital image. Enable "Tag Review" so you can approve any photos or posts others try to link to your profile before they appear on your timeline.
